JuliaPhylo

Julia phylogenetics task view

This is a curated index of Julia packages and binary wrappers related to phylogenetics, organized by tasks in a phylogenetic analysis workflow, following the structure of the CRAN Phylogenetics Task View.

Last reviewed: September 1, 2026

Overview

This view covers packages maintained by the JuliaPhylo organization, packages proposed in issue #1, directly relevant tools from the JuliaBioinformaticsJLLs catalogue, and packages found through a broader search of the Julia registry, GitHub, package organizations, and scientific software references.

Package descriptions use repository README files only. The JuliaPhylo label marks packages maintained by the JuliaPhylo organization. The No updates in past year label marks repositories whose default branch has no commits since September 1, 2025.

Scope

The focus is on Julia software -- Julia packages and command-line programs packaged as Julia artifacts -- that support modeling and analyses for phylogenetics and phylogeny-based evolutionary studies.

Working with phylogenetic data in Julia

Reading alignments and matrices

  • ClustalO_jll No updates in past year installs Clustal Omega, which reads sequences and profiles, writes multiple alignments in several formats, and reads or writes distance matrices and guide trees.
  • FAMSA_jll No updates in past year installs FAMSA, which reads FASTA inputs for multiple-sequence or profile-profile alignment and can import or export Newick guide trees and CSV distance matrices.
  • kalign_jll No updates in past year installs Kalign, which reads unaligned or aligned sequences and writes FASTA, MSF, or Clustal alignments.
  • MUSCLE_jll No updates in past year installs MUSCLE for multiple alignment of biological sequences and alternative alignment ensembles for comparing downstream tree predictions.
  • Goalign_jll No updates in past year installs Goalign for processing, converting, filtering, sampling, and summarizing multiple sequence alignments.
  • BioSequences.jl provides DNA, RNA, and amino-acid sequence types for biological sequence data.
  • FASTX.jl reads and writes FASTA and FASTQ files and provides utilities for those formats.
  • FastaIO.jl No updates in past year provides utilities for reading and writing FASTA files.
  • BioAlignments.jl provides sequence-alignment algorithms and data structures.
  • BioStockholm.jl No updates in past year parses Stockholm-format protein, RNA, and DNA multiple alignments.
  • BioSequenceMappings.jl manipulates alignments, calculates Hamming distances, and reads or writes FASTA files.
  • MIToS.jl works with protein multiple-sequence alignments and their evolutionary information.
  • ProgressiveAligner.jl No updates in past year builds a guide tree with neighbor joining, UPGMA, or WPGMA and progressively aligns protein sequences.
  • KmerGMA.jl No updates in past year searches FASTA databases for homologues of a reference gene family or sequence using k-mers.
  • Patchwork retrieves and concatenates phylogenetic markers and searches for homologues in distantly related taxa.

Reading trees

  • PhyloNetworks.jl JuliaPhylo reads and writes trees and networks in Newick and extended Newick formats.
  • NewickTree.jl parses Newick strings into tree structures and writes those trees back to Newick.
  • Gotree_jll No updates in past year installs Gotree for working with phylogenetic trees in formats including Newick, Nexus, and PhyloXML.
  • Phylo.jl No updates in past year reads phylogenetic trees from Newick and Nexus files.
  • PhyloTrees.jl No updates in past year provides tools for working with rooted phylogenetic trees.
  • PhyloTools.jl No updates in past year reads Newick trees for rooting, clustering, and plotting workflows.
  • TreeTools.jl reads and writes rooted phylogenetic or genealogical trees in Newick format.
  • MetaPhylo.jl No updates in past year parses Newick into phylogenetic tree structures built with Graphs and AbstractTrees.
  • TreeOfLife.jl No updates in past year parses and serializes cladograms and chronograms in Newick format.
  • LineagesIO.jl reads rooted trees and networks from Newick and phylogeny CSV sources.
  • PhyloBits.jl No updates in past year provides phylogenetic tree tables and a dependency-light PhyloNetworks tree reader.
  • NewickTreeTools.jl No updates in past year reads Newick trees for traversal, distance calculations, clustering, and output.
  • AsciiTrees.jl No updates in past year reads Newick phylogenetic trees and expands them as plain text.
  • TreeSim.jl represents, validates, traverses, and summarizes rooted trees for outbreak-modeling workflows.

Manipulating trees

  • PhyloNetworks.jl JuliaPhylo reroots and prunes phylogenies, removes hybrid edges, applies network rearrangements, and extracts displayed trees or networks.
  • AbstractTrees.jl provides child and descendant access plus preorder, postorder, and leaf traversal for tree-like structures.
  • NewickTree.jl provides tree nodes with traversal and query operations for Newick trees.
  • Gotree_jll No updates in past year installs Gotree for manipulating, annotating, rerooting, and reformatting phylogenetic trees.
  • Phylo.jl No updates in past year creates phylogenetic trees manually and supports operations on the resulting phylogenies.
  • Phylogenies.jl No updates in past year defines an abstract phylogeny interface, a concrete implementation, and higher-level methods.
  • PhyloTrees.jl No updates in past year provides node and branch operations, distances, and postorder traversal for rooted trees.
  • PhyloTools.jl No updates in past year midpoint-roots and clusters trees represented with NewickTree.jl.
  • TreeTools.jl navigates, prunes, reroots, traverses, and annotates rooted trees.
  • MetaPhylo.jl No updates in past year stores phylogenetic metadata and supports node and branch queries.
  • TreeOfLife.jl No updates in past year provides MRCA, monophyly, and other operations on cladograms and chronograms.
  • NewickTreeTools.jl No updates in past year traverses, cuts, clusters, and writes Newick trees.
  • TimeTrees.jl No updates in past year represents and manipulates fully resolved phylogenetic time trees, including non-contemporaneous leaves.
  • MCPhyloTree.jl No updates in past year supplies basic tree functionality for MCPhylo.jl.

Visualizing trees

  • PhyloMakie.jl JuliaPhylo plots PhyloNetworks trees and networks with Makie and supports edge-length scaling, gamma display, tip labels, colors, and widths.
  • PhyloPlots.jl JuliaPhylo plots PhyloNetworks trees and networks via R through RCall.
  • BasicTreePlots.jl provides Makie recipes for trees that implement the AbstractTrees interface.
  • NewickTree.jl includes a Plots recipe.
  • Gotree_jll No updates in past year installs Gotree for drawing and annotating phylogenetic trees from the command line.
  • Phylo.jl No updates in past year plots phylogenies and associated traits with Plots recipes.
  • PhyloTrees.jl No updates in past year provides a Plots recipe for rooted phylogenetic trees.
  • PhyloTools.jl No updates in past year plots NewickTree trees with Makie, including labels and orientation controls.
  • LineagesMakie.jl draws rooted lineage graphs with Makie using layouts, labels, clade annotations, and scale bars.
  • D3Trees.jl provides interactive D3 visualization for large trees with styles, tooltips, expansion, and lazy loading.
  • PalmTree.jl No updates in past year draws and annotates NewickTree trees with Luxor.
  • TikzQTrees.jl No updates in past year plots trees with TikzPictures and the LaTeX tikz-qtree package.
  • TreeRecipe.jl No updates in past year plots tree types that implement the AbstractTrees interface.
  • AsciiTrees.jl No updates in past year renders Newick phylogenetic trees as plain text.
  • TimeTrees.jl No updates in past year plots fully resolved phylogenetic time trees in interactive Julia sessions.

Comparing trees

  • PhyloDistances.jl computes tree distances and similarities, including Robinson-Foulds variants, quartet distance, Nye similarity, and Jaccard-Robinson-Foulds.
  • PhyloNetworks.jl JuliaPhylo compares phylogenetic trees and networks with dissimilarity measures.
  • Gotree_jll No updates in past year installs Gotree for comparing phylogenetic trees from the command line.
  • PhyloClustering.jl No updates in past year clusters phylogenetic trees with K-means, hierarchical clustering, Gaussian mixtures, or DBSCAN.
  • TreeTools.jl computes metrics for rooted phylogenetic and genealogical trees.
  • NewickTreeTools.jl No updates in past year computes network, patristic, and traversal distances from Newick trees.
  • PhylogeneticDistance.jl No updates in past year computes unweighted, weighted, variance-adjusted, and generalized UniFrac distance matrices.
  • SymbolicQuartetCF.jl studies identifiability of tree and network parameters with quartet concordance factors.

Summary statistics

  • PhyloNetworks.jl JuliaPhylo summarizes samples of phylogenetic networks and reports network features.
  • QuartetNetworkGoodnessFit.jl JuliaPhylo measures the goodness of fit of a phylogenetic network to data on four-taxon subsets.
  • Gotree_jll No updates in past year installs Gotree for summarizing phylogenetic trees from the command line.
  • PhyloClustering.jl No updates in past year summarizes structure among collections of phylogenetic trees with unsupervised learning.
  • Diversity.jl measures and partitions alpha, beta, and gamma diversity using phylogenetic or other similarities.
  • Nodiv.jl compares descendant-clade richness at every internal node against a null model.
  • TreeOfLife.jl No updates in past year calculates phylogenetic diversity, MRCA, and monophyly statistics.
  • TractableTimeTreeDistributions.jl No updates in past year calculates likelihoods, samples, credible regions, and point estimates for time-tree distributions.
  • PhylogeneticDistance.jl No updates in past year returns UniFrac distance or kernel matrices for microbiome comparisons.

Tree building in Julia

Simulating trees

  • PhyloCoalSimulations.jl JuliaPhylo simulates phylogenies under the network multispecies coalescent with possible inheritance correlation.
  • Phylo.jl No updates in past year generates random phylogenetic trees.
  • Coalescent.jl No updates in past year simulates coalescent genealogical histories conditioned on flexible demographic histories.
  • CoalescentTrees.jl No updates in past year simulates coalescent genealogies under standard and bounded models with heterochronous sampling support.
  • TreeProcesses.jl simulates forward birth-death and Yule trees and backward coalescent trees.
  • BirthDeathSimulation.jl No updates in past year simulates rooted time trees under a birth-death-shift process.
  • TreeTools.jl generates random trees with birth-death, Kingman, Yule, and simple-shape processes.

Inferring trees

  • NeighborJoining.jl reconstructs phylogenetic trees from pairwise distance matrices with regular, fast, or dynamic neighbor joining and converts results to Newick strings.
  • MolecularEvolution.jl No updates in past year performs branch-length and topology optimization for trees under user-defined evolutionary models.
  • SNaQ.jl JuliaPhylo estimates level-1 phylogenetic networks using maximum pseudolikelihood with numerical optimization of branch lengths and inheritance probabilities.
  • PhyLiNC.jl No updates in past year JuliaPhylo estimates phylogenetic networks from DNA sequences assuming sites evolve independently, but the method has not been thoroughly tested and NetRAX is recommended instead.
  • FastME_jll No updates in past year installs FastME for distance-based phylogeny reconstruction.
  • MrBayes_jll No updates in past year installs MrBayes for Bayesian inference of phylogeny.
  • PhyML_jll No updates in past year installs PhyML for maximum-likelihood tree inference from nucleotide or amino-acid alignments.
  • RAxML_jll No updates in past year installs standard RAxML v8 binaries for phylogenetic analysis and post-analysis of large phylogenies.
  • PhyloModels.jl No updates in past year calculates phylogenetic tree likelihoods with Felsenstein's pruning algorithm.
  • SubstitutionModels.jl No updates in past year provides biological-sequence substitution models for sequence evolution, phylogenetics, and simulation.
  • PhyNEST.jl No updates in past year reads multilocus alignments and estimates maximum-composite-likelihood species networks from four-taxon site patterns.
  • PhyloDiamond.jl No updates in past year learns four-node hybridization cycles in phylogenetic networks from concordance factors or gene trees.
  • InPhyNet.jl combines level-1 phylogenetic networks into a larger inferred network.
  • SymbolicQuartetCF.jl studies identifiability of phylogenetic tree and network parameters with quartet concordance factors.
  • SymbolicQuartetNetworkCoal.jl No updates in past year derives quartet concordance-factor formulas under the network multispecies coalescent for identifiability studies.
  • FourLeafMLE.jl No updates in past year estimates a four-leaf phylogenetic tree from DNA sequence data.
  • ProgressiveAligner.jl No updates in past year builds neighbor-joining, UPGMA, or WPGMA guide trees while progressively aligning protein sequences.
  • Whale.jl No updates in past year jointly infers gene-tree topologies and their reconciliations to a species tree.
  • DeadBird.jl No updates in past year performs Bayesian and maximum-likelihood inference for phylogenetic birth-death models of gene families.
  • PhylogeneticTrees.jl No updates in past year fits admixture trees with a mixed-integer quadratic optimization algorithm.
  • EvolutionModels.jl provides experimental DNA and protein CTMC models, simulation, likelihoods, and distances, but its README warns that results are unvalidated and not ready for production use.
  • MCPhylo.jl No updates in past year extends Mamba's Markov chain Monte Carlo sampling for Bayesian phylogenetic computations.
  • CAOS.jl No updates in past year generates characteristic-attribute rules from Newick or Nexus phylogenetic trees for sequence classification.

Adding a time dimension

  • TimeTrees.jl No updates in past year represents fully resolved phylogenetic time trees, including trees with non-contemporaneous leaves.
  • TreeOfLife.jl No updates in past year defines chronogram data types for time-scaled phylogenetic trees.
  • TractableTimeTreeDistributions.jl No updates in past year approximates time-tree distributions from MCMC posterior samples.
  • BirthDeathSimulation.jl No updates in past year represents rooted phylogenetic trees in units of time and simulates birth-death-shift trees.

Phylogenetic comparative methods

Ancestral state reconstruction

  • MolecularEvolution.jl No updates in past year performs ancestral inference under user-defined evolutionary models.
  • EvoTraits.jl performs ancestral reconstruction for continuous and discrete phylogenetic trait models.
  • AncestralSequenceReconstruction.jl No updates in past year reconstructs ancestral protein sequences with autoregressive generative and profile models.
  • PhyloInference.jl No updates in past year performs Bayesian inference for trait evolution on phylogenetic trees.

Trait evolution

  • PhyloTraits.jl JuliaPhylo analyzes continuous and discrete trait evolution on phylogenetic trees and networks.
  • PhyloGaussianBeliefProp.jl No updates in past year JuliaPhylo analyzes Gaussian models on phylogenetic networks and admixture graphs using belief propagation.
  • EvoTraits.jl fits continuous and discrete phylogenetic trait models and supports SIMMAP-aware analysis and model comparison.
  • Tapestree.jl analyzes diversification, trait, and biogeographic dynamics on phylogenies.
  • PhyloInference.jl No updates in past year provides Bayesian inference for trait evolution on phylogenetic trees.
  • Pagel.jl No updates in past year implements correlated-evolution analysis for discrete characters, but its README warns that it is a research prototype.
  • Phylo.jl No updates in past year evolves continuous and discrete traits on generated phylogenies.

Trait simulations

  • MolecularEvolution.jl No updates in past year simulates evolutionary states along trees under user-defined models.
  • SeqGen_jll No updates in past year installs Seq-Gen for Monte Carlo simulation of molecular sequence evolution along phylogenetic trees.
  • Phylo.jl No updates in past year evolves continuous and discrete traits on phylogenetic trees.
  • EvoTraits.jl simulates continuous and discrete traits under its comparative models.
  • PhyloModels.jl No updates in past year simulates genetic sequences along specified phylogenetic trees.
  • SubstitutionModels.jl No updates in past year supplies sequence-substitution models for evolutionary simulation.
  • PottsEvolver.jl simulates protein evolution with a Potts model along lineages or tree branches.
  • SeqSim.jl simulates sequences and alignments along trees and writes FASTA, Nexus, and PHYLIP output.
  • EvolutionModels.jl simulates DNA and protein sequences with experimental continuous-time Markov models.
  • Genie.jl No updates in past year simulates amino-acid or codon evolutionary trajectories with epistasis, codon bias, and selection.
  • SeqEvol No updates in past year evolves protein alignments with DCA-based Gibbs sampling and controllable selection pressure.

Diversification analysis

  • Tapestree.jl fits constant, diffused, fossilized, and occurrence birth-death models.
  • PANDA.jl No updates in past year implements macroevolutionary analyses on phylogenetic trees.
  • Pesto.jl fits state-dependent speciation and extinction models on reconstructed phylogenetic trees.
  • PhyBEARS.jl implements biogeographic state-dependent speciation and extinction models.
  • DeadBird.jl No updates in past year models comparative genomic count data with phylogenetic birth-death processes.

Phylogenetics in specific fields

Morphometrics

Time series and paleontology

  • Tapestree.jl fits fossilized and occurrence birth-death models for phylogenies with fossil information.
  • PaleobiologyDB.jl accesses fossil occurrences and taxonomy trees through the Paleobiology Database API.

Community and microbial ecology

  • Diversity.jl calculates phylogenetic diversity and partitions alpha, beta, and gamma diversity.
  • Nodiv.jl compares descendant-clade richness across cells against a null model at each internal phylogenetic node.
  • PhylogeneticDistance.jl No updates in past year computes UniFrac matrices for comparing microbiome taxonomic composition.
  • AssignTaxonomy.jl No updates in past year assigns taxonomic classifications to DNA sequences with an all-Julia RDP naive Bayesian classifier.

Phyloclimatic modeling

No package in the current scope has a README-supported entry for combining phylogenies with climate data.

Phylogeography and biogeography

  • Tapestree.jl fits models of biogeographic dynamics on phylogenies.
  • PhyBEARS.jl models biogeographic state-dependent speciation and extinction with systems of ODEs.
  • PopGen.jl provides population-genetics analysis in Julia.
  • GenomicDiversity.jl calculates genomic diversity, genetic distance, and population differentiation within and among populations.
  • OpenADMIXTURE.jl No updates in past year estimates ancestry by maximum likelihood from large SNP genotype datasets.
  • LocalAncestry.jl infers local ancestry from phased VCFs, reference ancestries, and a hidden Markov model.
  • PopGenCore.jl provides population-genetics data types and file I/O used by PopGen.jl.
  • PopGenSims.jl simulates related and unrelated individuals from population-specific allele frequencies.

Omics

  • CodonMolecularEvolution.jl No updates in past year is a descendant of MolecularEvolution.jl specializing in codon models, with a dN/dS catalogue currently consisting of FUBAR and difFUBAR.
  • MolecularEvolution.jl No updates in past year provides likelihood and ancestral analyses for user-defined molecular evolutionary models.
  • SubstitutionModels.jl No updates in past year defines DNA and protein substitution models for sequence evolution and phylogenetics.
  • PhyloModels.jl No updates in past year simulates genetic sequences and calculates phylogenetic tree likelihoods.
  • Whale.jl No updates in past year performs Bayesian gene-tree reconciliation and whole-genome duplication inference.
  • DeadBird.jl No updates in past year models comparative genomic count data, most commonly gene families, with phylogenetic birth-death processes.
  • ArDCA.jl No updates in past year learns autoregressive protein models with generalized logistic regression.
  • PottsEvolver.jl simulates protein evolution along lineages or tree branches with Potts models.
  • Genie.jl No updates in past year simulates amino-acid or codon sequence trajectories with mutation, epistasis, and selection.
  • SeqEvol No updates in past year evolves protein multiple-sequence alignments with DCA-based Gibbs sampling.
  • MIToS.jl extracts evolutionary information from protein multiple-sequence alignments.
  • KmerGMA.jl No updates in past year searches for homologous genes or sequences with a k-mer approach.
  • Patchwork retrieves and concatenates phylogenetic markers from whole-genome sequence data.

Gene tree–species tree and species delimitation

  • SNaQ.jl JuliaPhylo estimates level-1 phylogenetic networks using maximum pseudolikelihood under incomplete lineage sorting.
  • PhyloCoalSimulations.jl JuliaPhylo simulates phylogenies under the network multispecies coalescent with possible inheritance correlation.
  • QuartetNetworkGoodnessFit.jl JuliaPhylo checks the fit of a phylogenetic network to data from four-taxon subsets.
  • PhyNEST.jl No updates in past year estimates maximum-composite-likelihood species networks from multilocus alignments and site patterns.
  • PhyloDiamond.jl No updates in past year learns four-node hybridization cycles from concordance factors or gene-tree files.
  • InPhyNet.jl merges level-1 phylogenetic networks into a combined network.
  • SymbolicQuartetCF.jl studies identifiability of tree and network parameters from quartet concordance factors.
  • SymbolicQuartetNetworkCoal.jl No updates in past year derives quartet concordance-factor formulas under the network multispecies coalescent.
  • Whale.jl No updates in past year jointly infers gene trees and their reconciliation to a species tree.
  • DeadBird.jl No updates in past year infers gene-family duplication, loss, and gain processes on phylogenies.
  • PhyloPickMe.jl No updates in past year selects samples for inclusion in species-tree analyses.
  • PhylogeneticTrees.jl No updates in past year fits admixture trees using mixed-integer quadratic optimization.

Epidemiology

  • TreeKnit.jl No updates in past year infers ancestral reassortment graphs for segmented genomes such as human influenza.
  • Pathogen.jl No updates in past year fits individual-level infectious-disease models and infers who-infected-whom transmission pathways.
  • TreeSim.jl provides a rooted-tree layer for outbreak-modeling workflows.
  • glOpqua No updates in past year simulates pathogen epidemiology and evolution across scales.
  • Outbreaks.jl provides curated outbreak datasets, including contacts and pathogen DNA data.
  • Coalescent.jl No updates in past year simulates pathogen genealogical histories under flexible demographic and sampling histories.
